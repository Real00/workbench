# 工作台 Logo 第二版设计稿

使用内置 ImageGen 绘制，当前为概念稿，尚未替换应用中的图标。

参考：https://www.ibm.com/design/language/iconography/ui-icons/design/

规范依据：32px 网格、统一线宽、基础留白、简洁轮廓、小尺寸辨识。UI 图标规范用于约束清晰度；品牌图形仍需独立识别性。

方向：开放书页与工作空间的轮廓，单色蓝线，中文名称作为唯一字标。落地前需统一设计稿各处的路径细节，并单独检查 16/24/32px 的实际像素表现；图中的缩放示意不等同于完成该检查。

## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand
Create a refined brand design presentation for a Chinese personal productivity workspace named exactly “个人工作台”. This is a new design, completely unrelated to prior designs.
The application unites quick notes, task progress and a knowledge library. Its visual identity should express ONE open, orderly personal working space. Design an ORIGINAL minimalist outline emblem: two or at most three simple continuous geometric lines suggesting an open workspace or an open folio. Strong, elegant, memorable silhouette; generous negative space; uniform medium-thin stroke; consistent small rounded corners. Flat front view, based on a precise 32px grid, approximately 2px stroke at 32px. It must read clearly at 24px and work in a single color. Do not literally combine icons for the app's features.
Palette: cobalt blue #2563eb outline, dark ink #202939 Chinese wordmark, near-white #f6f7f9 background. NO filled square tile behind the emblem. NO gradient, shadow, texture, glow, 3D, person, head, arms, bowl, tray, roof, house, sparkles, central dot, decorative badge, Latin letters, or invented slogan.
Presentation: elegant restrained landscape design sheet, generous whitespace. Show ONE final coherent logo concept. Left: a large clean rendering of the standalone outline emblem. Right: the SAME emblem beside the exact Chinese wordmark “个人工作台” in carefully spaced modern Chinese sans-serif, showing the complete upper-left sidebar brand lockup on a very subtle white sidebar header strip. Below, show the same emblem at three small practical UI sizes, identical geometry, not alternative designs. No extra text, explanatory labels or fake UI controls. Focus on rigorous line design and understated visual identity, not presentation decoration.
